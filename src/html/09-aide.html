Help & shortcuts

Placing & moving

Place
Drag a thumbnail onto the plan. On a touch screen: tap the thumbnail, then tap the plan.
Move
Drag it by the centimetre. Its back sticks to a wall it nears, and it lines up with other furniture; Alt frees it of every magnet. Stuck to a wall by accident? Pull it away from the wall and it turns back the way it was.
Rotate
The handle above the selection (Shift = 15° steps), or Rotate 90°.
Rename / duplicate
Double-click the name to rename it. Duplicate (or Ctrl+D) copies the selection.

Walls

Draw
W (or the Wall button) arms it: click to start, click to place, it chains into the next segment. Digits then Enter place an exact length.
Magnets
Points snap to corners and existing walls, else to 0°, 45°, 90°. Shift keeps the two axes, Alt cuts every magnet.
Select
Click a wall: it gets its own card, and a disc, one handle per free end, plus × Delete, + Split and (on a crooked wall) an equerre to square it up appear on the wall itself.
Move
Pull a wall: the two rooms adjust together. A wall goes from one point to the other and stays there; an end that touches another wall follows it.
Length
On the wall's own card, type the exact length: it stretches the free end. A wall held at both ends has no end to give, and the field says so.

Doors & windows

Place
Drag one from the palette onto a wall: it orients itself and slides along it.
Turn
Rotate 90° flips the hinge or the leaf's swing; Flip side (or R) puts it on the other face.
Inspector
Sets width, depth, and the distance from the corner.
How it opens
A window's Opens buttons choose None, Single, or Double.

View

Zoom / pan
The wheel (or a pinch) zooms around the cursor; Space+drag, the right button, or two fingers pan.
Fit
Reframes on the whole flat.
Layers
The Furniture / Lighting / Sockets boxes show or hide each family.
Circulation
Shades the walkable floor and flags an overlap left in the way.

Lighting

The layer
The Circulation panel's Light box paints how lit each room is: warm where it reaches its target, dark and hatched below half of it.
Day / Night
Night counts the fixtures only. Day adds the windows, 2 000 lm per metre of opening.
Flux
A fixture radiates its type's default (ceiling light 1 500 lm, wall light 400, floor lamp 900). The inspector's Luminous flux field replaces it, and clearing the field puts the default back.
Target
Read off the room's name (kitchen 300 lx, office 500, bathroom 200, bedroom 100, corridor 100, otherwise 150). The room's own Light target field wins over the name.
What it says
A wall stops the light: a room is lit by its own fixtures, never by its neighbour's. Furniture casts no shadow here.

Together

Cursors
The other person's cursor and selection show up live.
Chat
The 💬 button, to talk inside the plan.
Sync
Changes are shared automatically; the status chip in the bar says so.
